JECKER ANDRE helps operations teams keep daily work moving with focused boards for shift scheduling, patient work files, task management, and container tracking.

Create local records with an owner, due detail, status, priority, and notes. Use the daily dashboard to review urgent work, open coverage, patient follow-up, and container items that need action.

The app works without account registration, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, external APIs, or Android permissions. Records stay in local app storage on the device.
