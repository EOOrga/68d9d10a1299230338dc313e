Patient files

Workflow

  • Create a local record for the shift, patient file, task, or container.
  • Assign an owner, due detail, priority, and current status.
  • Review the priority board and update work as it moves forward.

Privacy first

JECKER ANDRE stores records on the device. It does not request Android permissions, does not use APIs, and does not send records to JECKER ANDRE or third parties.
